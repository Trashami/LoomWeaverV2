Loom Nordic Runes, Loom Elven, and Loom Ring Script
Original LoomWeaver numeral designs, copyright 2026 LoomWeaver.

Outlines are authored in scripts/build_dice_fantasy_fonts.py. These are original
fantasy-inspired Arabic numeral fonts, not fonts extracted from a game or film.
They contain digits, a period, and a space for use on dice faces. The generator
and fonts may be used and modified as part of LoomWeaver.

Build dependencies: fonttools and brotli (development only).
