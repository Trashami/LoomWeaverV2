PHB Downtime Crafting (p. 187)

You can craft non-magical objects, including adventuring equipment and works of art. You must be proficient with the artisan's tools related to the object.

  • Cost: Expend raw materials worth half the total market value of the item.
  • Progress: For each day of downtime spent, you complete up to 5 gp of market value.
  • Collaboration: Multiple characters proficient in the required tools can combine their efforts to divide the crafting time.

XGE Downtime Crafting (p. 128)

Xanathar's Guide to Everything introduces weekly work blocks for nonmagical items:

  • Workweeks: Divide gold piece cost by 50 to find the required workweeks (e.g. 50 gp = 1 workweek).
  • Herbalism Kit: Crafting Antitoxin and Potions of Healing requires proficiency with an Herbalism Kit and 25 gp of raw materials per basic healing potion.
  • Tool Synergies: Leatherworker's tools for hide/leather armor, Smith's tools for metal weapons and heavy armor, Alchemist's supplies for alchemical items.

XGE Magic Items & Formulas (p. 128)

Creating a magic item requires a specific Formula (schematic), appropriate spells or slots, and an exotic monster component:

  • Common: 50 gp · 1 workweek · CR 1–3 Creature Component
  • Uncommon: 200 gp · 2 workweeks · CR 4–8 Creature Component
  • Rare: 2,000 gp · 10 workweeks · CR 9–12 Creature Component
  • Very Rare: 20,000 gp · 25 workweeks · CR 13–18 Creature Component

DMG Wilderness Foraging (p. 111)

Characters travelling at normal or slow pace can forage for sustenance by making a Wisdom (Survival) check:

  • DC 10: Abundant food and water sources (lush forests, coastal shallows).
  • DC 15: Limited sources (sparse grasslands, rocky foothills).
  • DC 20: Very little sources (arid deserts, deep subterranean caves).
  • Yield: On success, yields 1d6 + WIS modifier pounds of food and 1d6 + WIS modifier gallons of water per day.
